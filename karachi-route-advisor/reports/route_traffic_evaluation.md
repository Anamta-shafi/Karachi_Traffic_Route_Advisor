# Route-Specific Traffic Model Evaluation

> **Synthetic-target warning:** route traffic labels are generated by a documented simulation. These metrics measure recovery of the simulation policy and do not represent real-world Karachi traffic prediction accuracy.

- Model version: `route-traffic-sim-v1`
- Selected model: `random_forest`
- Target: LOW / MODERATE / HIGH simulated route congestion
- Label policy: low: delay_ratio < 0.18; moderate: 0.18 <= delay_ratio < 0.45; high: delay_ratio >= 0.45
- Synthetic training rows: 74,500
- Real external reference records: 347,285 (UCI traffic 40,575; STATS19 306,710)
- Karachi route-level traffic records: 0

## Data Provenance

- Traffic features: real OpenStreetMap Gulistan-e-Jauhar drive graph.
- Traffic labels: synthetic BPR-style route simulation.
- Time-of-week calibration: real external UCI 492 Minnesota traffic volume and UK STATS19 collision times.
- Accident risk display: real external UK STATS19 collision-time prior.
- No observed Karachi route/segment traffic data was used.

## Limitations

- This is not a validated real-world Karachi traffic forecast.
- The synthetic labels encode the simulator's assumptions.
- The external profiles are geographically external to Karachi.
- The model does not change custom A* route scoring.
